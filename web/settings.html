With several players at the table, "stand" means open that player now and "draw" means keep drawing against them. Each player is settled against whatever the banker holds when they're opened, so this table is also the order to open people in.

Where the banker's edge comes from

The rules are nearly symmetric. Two busts push, so the casino's usual edge (you bust first and lose anyway) is gone. Against a fixed banker that leaves the edge with you, because you make real choices and he doesn't.

The smart banker wins it back with information. He plays last and can count your face-down cards; you see nothing of his. Two cards and standing means 16 to 20. Five cards means 五龙 or a bust. That knowledge is worth about 5.7% per hand.

Opening people early is how he cashes it in. He settles the players he has nothing to gain from drawing against, like five-card hands, then keeps drawing against the rest. A bust after that only costs him against the players still waiting.

The player-only 2× five-card bust adds about 2% more for the banker.
