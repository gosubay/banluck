The rules we simulate

Banluck is the blackjack Singaporean and Malaysian families play over Chinese New Year. House rules vary from table to table. This is the exact set the solver uses.

The Ace shrinks as your hand grows

This is the rule that makes Banluck strategy different from casino blackjack.

You drew a 5 and your total went down from 20 to 16. That never happens in a casino.

One hand, start to finish

  1. DealTwo cards each, all face down.
  2. Bonus checkBan-Ban or Ban-Luck is shown and paid. The hand ends.
  3. You drawHit to at least 16, then hit or stand. Five cards max. Bust in silence.
  4. Banker drawsSees only how many cards you hold. Must also reach 16.
  5. RevealBoth hands flip and settle on the rankings above.

Settings fixed for the solver
  • One player against the banker, 1-unit bets.
  • A fresh 52-card deck every hand, so card-removal odds are exact.
  • Fixed banker: hits below 16, stands on any 16 or more.
  • Smart banker: sees your card count and plays to hurt you most. Solved as a two-player game with CFR+, to within 0.002% per hand.
  • The five-card bust penalty is for players only. A banker who busts on five cards pays 1×.
  • Your strategy uses only your own cards.
Not modelled
  • Several players at one table. A real banker can reveal some players at 16 or 17, then keep drawing against the rest. The smart banker captures the core of that: playing differently by how many cards you hold.
  • Playing several hands from one deck without reshuffling (card counting).
